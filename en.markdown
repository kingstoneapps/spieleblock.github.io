# Spieleblock
## Keep score digitally – simple, fast and clear

Spieleblock replaces pen and paper for writing down game scores. Create individual players or groups of players, enter scores quickly and clearly, and keep track of past games.

The app is ideal for a relaxed game night or regular rounds with friends and family when there is no pen within reach. Thanks to the built-in history you can look up earlier results at any time. The high score list shows at a glance who usually comes out on top.

# Privacy policy for the app "Spieleblock"
This privacy policy informs you about the nature, scope and purpose of the processing of personal data within my mobile app "Spieleblock".

### 1. Controller
The controller responsible for data processing within the meaning of the GDPR is:
+ Robert Königstein
+ E-mail: kingstoneapps@gmail.com

### 2. Types of data processed
The app only processes data that you enter yourself or that is created locally on your device by using the app:
+ Player names (pseudonyms)
+ Scores and game history
+ App settings (e.g. chosen design, chosen language)
+ Feedback content (only if you actively use the feedback function)

### 3. Purpose of processing and legal basis
The data is processed solely to provide the functions of the app (recording game results).
+ Providing the app's functions: the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract or user relationship).
+ Feedback function: if you send me feedback by e-mail, I process your e-mail address and the content of the message in order to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

### 4. Local storage, device backup and data sharing
The app works offline and has no access to the internet. All game data you enter is stored by the app locally in a database on your device.
+ No data is transmitted to me or to my servers.
+ **Android device backup (Google account):** The app takes part in the backup function of the Android operating system. If you have enabled backup on your device (Android settings, section "Backup"), Android automatically stores the app's data (players, games, scores and settings) in your Google account and restores it when the app is reinstalled or when you move to a new device. This backup is not carried out by me but by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) in accordance with its privacy policy; I have no access to it. You can switch off the backup at any time in your device settings and delete data that has already been backed up there.
+ **Export and import:** With the export function you can store a backup file yourself in a place of your choice (e.g. cloud storage or e-mail) and read it back in with the import function. You alone decide where this file goes.
+ Beyond that, no data is passed on to third parties.

### 5. Storage period
The data remains on your device until:
1. you delete the data via the app's settings, or
2. you uninstall the app from your device.

If you have enabled Android's device backup, a copy of the data may remain in your Google account until you switch off or delete the backup in the settings of your device or your Google account. After a reinstallation Android may restore this copy.

Feedback e-mails are deleted as soon as the purpose of the communication has been fulfilled and no statutory retention obligations prevent this.

### 6. Your rights
You have the following rights towards me with regard to personal data concerning you:
+ Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
+ Right to rectification or erasure (Art. 16/17 GDPR)
+ Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
+ Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
+ Right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority about my processing of your personal data.

### 7. Changes to this privacy policy
I reserve the right to adapt this privacy policy so that it always meets current legal requirements or to reflect changes to the app's functions.
